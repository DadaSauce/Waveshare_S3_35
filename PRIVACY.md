# Privacy Policy - ClockNest Home Hub

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

ClockNest Home Hub is a personal hobby project used only by its owner on
devices in the owner's home.

## Information the app uses

When you sign in with Google, the app gets permission (the Smart Device
Management scope) to read your Nest thermostat's status - temperature,
humidity, mode, set point and heating/cooling state - and to change its set
point when you tap the controls on the device.

## How it is stored

- The sign-in token is stored only on your own devices (the clock's flash
  memory, the Kindle's local storage, and the owner's computer keychain).
- Thermostat readings are shown on the screen and kept in memory only.
- Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

## Sharing

No information is sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone. It is not
used for advertising or any purpose other than showing and controlling your
thermostat.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
Deleting the token on the device has the same effect locally.
